Privacy Policy: Personal Sleep Metrics

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Purpose and scope

Personal Sleep Metrics is a personal project under development to retrieve and analyze the owner's own Oura Ring and Polar watch metrics. It is not currently offered to other users.

Data accessed

When authorized by the owner, the application is intended to access sleep and recovery metrics from Oura and Polar. It may also process account identifiers and authorization tokens needed to retrieve those metrics.

Use of data

Data is intended for personal analysis and CSV export. It is not sold or used for advertising.

Storage and retention

The application is not yet deployed, and its final storage and retention arrangements have not been established. This policy will be updated to describe those arrangements before the application begins collecting data. Health records and authorization credentials will not be published in this website's public repository.

Access and deletion

The owner controls whether to connect their accounts. Before collection begins, this policy will describe how to disconnect integrations and delete stored data and credentials.

Website hosting

This informational website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes visitor information under its own Privacy Statement .
